# DoctorDashboard Referral Feature

You are an assistant helping develop the Patient Referral feature for DoctorDashboard, a React Native/Expo app for general physicians to manage patient care and refer to specialists.

## Feature Overview

Implement a referral modal that allows general physicians to refer patients to specialists and therapists. The feature should support transferring patients to specialists in various departments and include referral notes/recommendations.

## Specialist Departments

Users can refer patients to the following specialist departments (excluding General Medicine):

- **Cardiology** - Heart and cardiovascular conditions
- **Pediatrics** - Children's health and development
- **Orthopedics** - Bone, joint, and musculoskeletal conditions
- **Dermatology** - Skin conditions and diseases
- **Internal Medicine** - Complex internal organ conditions
- **Surgery** - Surgical interventions
- **Psychiatry** - Mental health and behavioral conditions
- **Neurology** - Nervous system and neurological conditions
- **Oncology** - Cancer treatment
- **Gastroenterology** - Digestive system conditions
- **Pulmonology** - Respiratory and lung conditions
- **Rheumatology** - Autoimmune and joint disorders
- **Endocrinology** - Hormonal and metabolic disorders
- **Nephrology** - Kidney and renal conditions
- **Urology** - Urinary system conditions

## UI/UX Requirements

1. **Referral Modal** should display:
   - Patient name and ID
   - Tab selection: "To Doctor" / "To Therapist"
   - Specialist department dropdown
   - Referral reason/notes textarea
   - Urgency level selector (Normal, Urgent, Emergency)
   - Submit and Cancel buttons

2. **Referral Submission** should:
   - Validate required fields
   - Store referral with timestamp
   - Include referring physician name
   - Generate referral ID/code
   - Show success confirmation

3. **Referral History** should display:
   - List of sent referrals
   - Status (Pending, Accepted, Completed, Rejected)
   - Specialist department and name
   - Date and urgency level
